### RoCar - Car Price Predictor based on Machine Learning

- Dates: 03/2024 - 06/2024
- Link: https://github.com/hutanmihai/bachelor-thesis

This thesis introduces a production-grade web application, tailored for the Romanian automotive
market, designed to predict vehicle prices. RoCar utilizes a model specialized in Romania's economy
and pricing, trained on data we have independently scraped. The application stands out by using both
structured data (year of production, manufacturer, model, options) and information from images and
descriptions, leveraging a self-made multimodal architecture for more accurate predictions.
